Q. 5% tax when unstaking or what?
A. Distribute stake/lend amounts to all 3 but when unstaking take stakedAmount from Treasury and rewardAmount from ROI and cut 5% from stakedAmount
Q. Is the price calculated from the total amount currently in the treasury, ROI, HQ + Simulated?
A. Yes, that’s correct. I am describing it like this:
   NAL = Newly Added Liquidity (The amount new users want to purchase STABL3 with)
   NCS = Newly Added Circulating Supply (The quantity of STABL3 users receive when purchasing that is newly entering into circulation)
   Market Price = (Total Current Liquidity + NAL) / (Circulating Supply + NCS)
