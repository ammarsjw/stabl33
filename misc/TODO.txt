Q. 5% tax when unstaking or what?
A. Distribute stake/lend amounts to all 3 but when unstaking take stakedAmount from Treasury and rewardAmount from ROI and cut 5% from stakedAmount
Q. Is the price calculated from the total amount currently in the treasury, ROI, HQ + Simulated?
A. Yes, that’s correct. I am describing it like this:
   NAL = Newly Added Liquidity (The amount new users want to purchase STABL3 with)
   NCS = Newly Added Circulating Supply (The quantity of STABL3 users receive when purchasing that is newly entering into circulation)
   Market Price = (Total Current Liquidity + NAL) / (Circulating Supply + NCS)

Clarification needed:
Borrowing:
- A user can't payback/exchange more than the UCD they themselves have explicitly borrowed.
- "Leftover" Stabl3 is calculated at the end of their borrowing, e.g. given a user borrows 1 UCD each day for 100 days, until and unless they payback/exchange all 100 of their UCD the "leftover" Stabl3 will be unknown.
   ~ The logic here being that it is too computationally expensive to keep track of all rates that they borrowed at and then try and find the rate difference.
   ~ e.g. if said user tries to payback 76 UCD, I would then have to compare and consider the rate at which the user borrowed for each UCD individually.
   ~ Trying to find a methodology to somehow maintain an average of rates borrowed at would also be near impossible
